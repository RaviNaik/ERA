    04 / 10 · TASK 2

    Disable bias correction — when does it stop mattering?

    c(t) = √(1 − β₂ᵗ) / (1 − β₁ᵗ) → 1 as t → ∞ — the ratio of the corrected step to the uncorrected one. c(t) is non-monotonic for β₂ ≤ 0.99 — it overshoots, dips below 1, then climbs back — so "stops mattering" means it enters the band and stays.

    Steps until |c(t)−1| enters the band and stays
