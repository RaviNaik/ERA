Phase 4

Packing Policies — All 5 Strategies

Each policy is evaluated on packing utilization, boundary risk, and correctness of loss/attention/position masks. Structure-preserving is used in production.

Three Masks Per Batch

LM

Which tokens contribute to the gradient. Tool observations and pad tokens are zeroed out.

ATT

Causal attention within each document's boundary. No cross-document attention leakage.

POS
